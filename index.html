Privacy Policy

Arrow Maze · Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Maze (the “Game”, “App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and shares information when you play the Game on your mobile device. By downloading or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

Arrow Maze is a free puzzle game. It is supported by advertising provided by third-party services, Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which are described below.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide or that is created in the Game

  • Player name and avatar: when you start the Game, a random nickname (for example “SwiftTiger42”) and an avatar emoji are generated automatically. You may change the nickname in Settings. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as a nickname.
  • Player ID: a randomly generated identifier (for example “AM-7K2Q9X”) that identifies your player profile. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Gameplay data: your current level, stars, score (weekly, monthly and total), hints and settings (sound, vibration, theme).

This data is stored locally on your device. If the online leaderboard feature is available in your version of the Game, your nickname, avatar, Player ID, level and scores may be sent to our servers so that they can be shown on the weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards. Other players can see this information on the leaderboard.

2.2 Information collected automatically by advertising services

When ads are shown, the advertising partners may automatically collect and process the following, according to their own privacy policies:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and software information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, mobile carrier)
  • App usage and ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, installs, performance and fraud-prevention data)
  • Crash and diagnostic data related to the ad SDKs

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or SMS messages.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, maintain and improve the Game (saving progress, showing leaderboards).
  • To display ads and keep the Game free to play.
  • To measure ad performance and detect fraud or abuse.
  • To fix bugs, monitor stability and respond to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Advertising Choices

  • Personalized ads: ads shown in the Game may be personalized based on your advertising ID and interests. You can opt out of personalized ads on Android at Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required by law, the Game asks for your consent through a consent message before showing personalized ads. You may change your choice at any time if the Game offers a privacy options button, or by clearing the app data.
  • Unity Ads: you can manage your Unity privacy preferences at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With advertising partners (AdMob and Unity Ads) as described above;
  • With other players, limited to your nickname, avatar, Player ID, level and score on the leaderboard;
  • With service providers who help us operate the Game, under confidentiality obligations;
  • When required by law, court order or to protect our rights, users or the public;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, or the minimum age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below this age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information, contact us at the email below and we will delete it. Because the Game contains advertising, we recommend that parents supervise younger players.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data (progress, settings, Player ID) stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game.
  • Leaderboard data (if stored on our servers) is kept while your profile is active and in any case only as long as needed to run the leaderboards. Weekly and monthly scores reset periodically.
  • To request deletion of your leaderboard data, email us your Player ID at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Data held by AdMob and Unity is governed by their own retention policies.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal data. To exercise any right, contact us at the email below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. International Transfers
